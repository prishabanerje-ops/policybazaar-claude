## The 4 factors — verdict at a glance

| Factor | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Fewer Europeans | ❌ Not supported — flat, Western Europe up |
| More Indian/Subcontinent | ❌ Share flat 52-55%, not rising — but conversion gap below is real |
| Pocket size / cash-conscious | ❌ Not supported — mix and conversion stable all 8 months |
| PED leads declined | ❌ **Opposite is true** — share rose 5.3% → 7.3% |

---

## Top 3 to act on

1. **Indian-subcontinent conversion gap — confirmed, every month.** Converts 15.6-24% vs. 23-43% for European-origin leads. 8 for 8 months, no exceptions.
2. **PED is growing, not shrinking.** Share up 5.3% → 7.3%, and carries 3.5-9x normal ticket size (APE). Protect and grow this segment — don't try to "recover" it.
3. **A marketing channel vanished — WhatsApp.** 2,185-3,105 leads/month sitewide, Jan-Apr → **zero every month since May**. Explained: WhatsApp is banned for insurance solicitation in the UAE, so this was a regulatory-driven shutdown, not a tracking break. Still the single biggest volume event in the investigation — worth confirming what replaced this channel, if anything.

---

## Not real problems (checked, ruled out)

- Europe's lead share isn't shrinking (flat ~6% all year)
- Income/salary mix hasn't shifted toward lower bands
- The "West" (US/UK/CA/AU) decline isn't a clean trend — it's the WhatsApp loss + a normal seasonal dip + one unexplained June spike, not a steady slide
