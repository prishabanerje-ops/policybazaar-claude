# **Neurology Cover in the UAE: Consultation, Scans & Long-term Care**

For any medical issues affecting your brain, spinal cord, and nervous system, immediately seek consultation from a neurologist. Neurology is a medical specialty that treats the nervous system. From migraines to coordinating long-term care for stroke, epilepsy, and multiple sclerosis, neurologists take care of everything. 

However, neurological diagnostics and treatment can involve advanced imaging, specialist medication, and (sometimes) hospitalization. Since all this can be costly, people often want to know the neurologist consultation cost and insurance cover availability in the UAE.

**Quick answer:** A neurologist consultation in Dubai typically costs AED 500–1,500 for an initial visit, and most UAE health insurance plans cover it in-network. Basic plans usually require a GP referral first; semi-comprehensive and comprehensive plans allow more direct specialist access. Your exact co-pay depends on your insurer and plan tier.

## **What Does a Neurologist Do?**

A neurologist is a medical professional who manages and treats disorders of the brain, spinal cord, nerves, and muscles. They have several responsibilities, including:

* Identifying symptoms of seizures, memory loss, numbness, tremors, or chronic headaches  
* Suggesting neurological tests, including MRI, CT, and EEG scans and managing chronic neurological conditions  
* Coordinating with neurosurgeons, physiotherapists, and rehabilitation specialists to treat the core issue


## **What are the Sub-specialties of Neurology?**

Neurology covers a wide range of focus areas. Here are the major ones: 

* Stroke & Vascular Neurology  
* Epilepsy & Seizure Disorders  
* Multiple Sclerosis & Neuroimmunology  
* Movement Disorders, such as Parkinson's disease  
* Headache & Migraine Medicine  
* Neuromuscular Disorders  
* Paediatric Neurology  
* Neurocritical Care

If you're facing any nerve disorder, consult a doctor. Moreover, before finalizing a doctor, check whether their sub-specialization aligns with your specific concern. 

## **What are the Common Neurological Conditions?**

Some of the most frequently diagnosed neurological conditions in the UAE include:

* **Headache:** Migraine and chronic headache disorders  
* **Parkinson's disease:** A chronic disorder  
* **Epilepsy:** Seizures due to abnormal brain activity  
* **Stroke:** Interrupted blood flow to the brain  
* **[Multiple sclerosis (MS)](/health-insurance/health-guide/multiple-sclerosis/):** An autoimmune condition  
* **Peripheral neuropathy:** Damage to nerves causing numbness  
* **[Alzheimer's disease](/health-insurance/health-guide/alzheimer-disease/):** A condition affecting memory and thinking  
* **Dementia:** A decline in memory, reasoning and other cognitive abilities  
* **Meningitis:** Inflammation of the membranes around the brain and spinal cord


**Key point**: Seek a specialist consultation immediately if you notice signs like experiencing frequent headaches, loss of memory, or numbness.

## **What are the Neurological Treatments and Procedures?**

Neurological treatments fall into two categories: surgical and non-surgical. Here is an overview of both types:

### **Non-Surgical**

**Medication Management**  
Anti-seizure drugs, MS-modifying therapies, migraine preventives

**Diagnostic Imaging & Testing**  
MRI, CT scans, and EEG to identify the problem

**Physical & Cognitive Rehabilitation**  
Physiotherapy and cognitive therapy following stroke or nerve injury

**Lifestyle & Trigger Management**  
Sleep, stress, and dietary adjustments for migraine and epilepsy patients

### **Surgical & Advanced Interventions**

**Stroke Intervention**  
Clot-dissolving medication or mechanical thrombectomy

**Deep Brain Stimulation**  
For movement disorders, like advanced Parkinson's disease

**Epilepsy Surgery**  
Considered when seizures don't respond to medication

**Neuro-oncology Procedures**  
Surgical removal or targeted therapy for brain and spinal tumours

## **When Should You Consult a Neurologist?**

Many patients are referred to a neurologist by a general practitioner after initial symptoms are flagged. However, in some situations it is worth consulting a specialist at the earliest:

* Warning Signs   
  Sudden weakness or slurred speech  
    
* Seizures  
  Sudden seizure  
    
* Headaches  
  Constant headaches, especially with vision changes  
    
* Memory and Confusion  
  Unexplained memory loss or confusion  
    
* Movement and Balance Issues   
  Tremors or difficulty with maintaining balance


## **What Medical Qualifications You Must Check Before Consultation?**

Before scheduling a consultation, make sure the neurologist is licensed to practice in the UAE by the appropriate health authority. Doctors in Dubai are licensed by the DHA, those in Abu Dhabi by the DOH, while MOHAP oversees licensing in the Northern Emirates.

Also look at the doctor's specific area of neurology. Their professional experience and sub-specialization within the field can also help you choose the right specialist.

## **Confirm Your Neurology Cover** 

Review your health insurance policy to understand which neurology-related consultations and treatments are covered:

**Verify the Network Hospitals**  
Check your [insurer's network list](/health-insurance/network-hospitals/) to see whether the hospital or clinic covers your condition's consultation, treatment, and diagnostic tests. 

**Know Your Plan Level**  
The type of health insurance plan you have may determine how you can access a neurologist. Under a basic plan, you need to consult a GP before seeing a specialist, and [outpatient (OPD) consultations](/health-insurance/health-guide/opd-cover/) may be subject to a sub-limit. A semi-comprehensive plan may provide direct access to specialists at clinics, while comprehensive coverage allows you to visit a hospital-based specialist.

**Check the Covered Treatments**  
If a GP refers you to a neurologist at a clinic or hospital, confirm that your medical problem, diagnostic tests and recommended treatment are covered by your policy. It's always better to verify your benefits before starting any treatment, even if you have comprehensive insurance.

**Review Co-payments and Deductibles**  
Check whether your policy requires you to pay a **co-payment or deductible** towards the consultation, tests, or treatment.

## **Does Health Insurance Cover Neurology Treatment?**

Yes. Since neurology is considered a core medical specialty and medically necessary, most plans, including basic, semi-comprehensive, and comprehensive health insurance plans in the UAE, cover it. However, some neurological conditions may fall under pre-existing conditions, which may have a waiting period in some cases. Always check this with your insurer.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

**Does insurance cover the neurologist consultation cost in the UAE?**   
Yes, all insurance policies include in-network neurologist consultations. However, the co-payment and sub-limits of certain coverage may vary as per the plan tier, that is, basic and enhanced plans. Basic plans typically route you through a GP referral first, while comprehensive plans allow more direct specialist access, so check your policy wording for the exact terms.

**Is multiple sclerosis treatment in Dubai covered by health insurance?**   
Multiple sclerosis may or may not be covered in your policy. The coverage varies by insurer and plan you choose. If it is covered, further check for the coverage limits, pre-approval requirements, and annual caps. Some plans may also treat MS as a pre-existing condition, which can affect your waiting period, so confirm this directly with your insurer.

**Is EEG covered by insurance in the UAE?**   
Yes, in most cases. EEGs suggested by doctors to investigate seizures or diagnose other similar symptoms are covered under UAE health insurance plans. Coverage is usually subject to your plan's annual diagnostics limit, and some insurers may require pre-approval before the test is booked.

**Is a brain MRI covered under health insurance in Dubai?**   
Yes, diagnostic tests, like X-rays, MRIs, and CT scans, are included in all types of medical insurance plans. However, you need to check with your insurance provider or read the policy document to know the coverage amount, specifically for your brain MRI.

**Is epilepsy medication covered under UAE health insurance?**   
Generally, epilepsy medication is covered under medical insurance policies. However, the exact amount of coverage depends on your plan's annual pharmacy limit and co-payment structure. Long-term anti-seizure therapy can sometimes fall under a separate chronic-medication sub-limit, so it's worth confirming this with your insurer before you start treatment.

**Which health insurance plans cover stroke, cancer, and heart attack treatment in the UAE?**   
Most comprehensive UAE health insurance plans, and dedicated critical-illness add-ons, cover stroke treatment alongside cancer and heart attack care, since all three are treated as major medical events rather than routine claims. Coverage typically includes hospitalization, diagnostics, and rehabilitation, though annual limits, network restrictions, and pre-existing-condition rules still apply, so confirm the specifics with your insurer.
