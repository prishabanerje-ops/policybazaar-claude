---

## Why this matters now

Google's AI Overview now answers questions like "best builders in Ahmedabad" directly on the search page, before any of the usual blue links. It names a short list of sources as it answers.

If a brand isn't on that short list, it's invisible at the exact moment someone is deciding who to trust, before a click, before a site visit, before a lead ever reaches the sales team.

This is a new front door to the business, and it runs on different rules than the search results Goyal & Co has been ranking in for years.

## What's already working

All three of the questions above are Ahmedabad, trust-and-quality questions:

- "best luxury apartment builders in Ahmedabad"
- "most trusted real estate developers in Gujarat"
- "best real estate companies for commercial office space in Ahmedabad"

That's a real foundation. The goal is to extend it to pricing, regulation, and the Bengaluru market.

## Zero visibility in Bengaluru, where the pipeline is growing

**0** of the Bengaluru / Karnataka questions tested cited Goyal & Co.

Every Bengaluru- and Karnataka-market question in this sample (pricing in Devanahalli, plotted development cost, land title checks) came back with no mention of Goyal & Co. This is despite an active project pipeline there, including Goyal Orchid Nirvana and Goyal Orchid Greens. The Ahmedabad reputation isn't yet carrying over to the newer market.

---

## Why: a scale gap in domain authority

| Domain | Authority score | Backlinks | Referring domains | Monthly visits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Goyal & Co** | **30** | **1.4K** | **390** | **19.2K** |
| DLF | 55 | 28.2K | 2.6K | 88.1K |
| Godrej Properties | 51 | 14.9K | 2.9K | 350K |
| Lodha Group | 55 | 24.5K | 2.8K | 320K |

*Source: SEMrush Backlink Analytics.*

This is what search engines and AI systems lean on when picking which sources to trust and cite. Goyal & Co trails these three by roughly 10 to 20 times on backlinks and referring domains, and by 4 to 18 times on monthly visits. This is a correlation worth acting on, not a proven single cause.

---

## What this means in practice

Pricing questions, NRI investment questions, and regulation questions are exactly where a serious buyer makes up their mind. All of them went uncited in this sample.

Each of those moments is a chance for a competitor, or a listing portal, to be the name a buyer remembers instead of Goyal & Co.

---

## The opportunity

AI Overview isn't dominated by any single developer yet, only by listing portals. That's an open field.

Closing even part of the authority and structure gap could move Goyal & Co from a brand that's sometimes named to the first developer cited for its home market.

---

## Scope: what this covers, and what's next

This is an initial 25-question diagnostic sample, out of a planned set of 50, covering Ahmedabad and Bengaluru buyer intent.

It's enough to see the pattern clearly. A fuller pass, including the brand-specific and head-to-head comparison questions not yet run, would sharpen the picture further and is recommended before finalizing any plan.
