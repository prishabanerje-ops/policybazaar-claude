## The two problems, and they are opposites

**Problem 1. Where we already rank on page 1, nobody clicks.**

The hub ranks in the **top 10** for a cluster of "corporate" queries and gets **zero clicks** from any of them.

| Query | Position | Impressions | Clicks |
|---|---|---|---|

| affordable group health insurance for 10 employees | **7.5** | 205 | **0** |

| best corporate health insurance plans | **9.1** | 121 | **0** |

That is **1,547 impressions from top-10 positions returning nothing.** Across all queries where the hub ranks top 10, it collects 2,506 impressions and **4 clicks**.

Here is why. The hub's title is:

> Group Health Insurance Dubai & UAE | Compare Plans from AED 2/Day*

**The word "corporate" appears nowhere in the title, and in none of the 31 headings on the page.** We rank fifth for "corporate health insurance" and the searcher sees a result that does not say "corporate". This is a copy problem, not a ranking problem, and it is the single highest-value writing task in the segment.

**Problem 2. Everywhere else, our own pages compete with each other.**

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Queries where 2 or more of our Group Health pages rank | **520 of 1,774** |
| Share of segment impressions on those queries | **88%** |
| Worst case | **14 of our own pages on one query** |

Example, `group health insurance uae`, 2,141 impressions and 0 clicks:

| Position | Impressions | Page |
|---|---|---|
| 22.4 | 722 | small-businesses article |
| 34.0 | 673 | **the hub** |

Five pages, none on page 1, and the small-businesses article outranks the hub for a query the hub should own. The same pattern holds for `group medical insurance` (6 pages), `group medical insurance uae` (10 pages), `group insurance uae` (13 pages) and `group health insurance dubai` (14 pages).

**So the job this month is: make the hub unmistakably the page for these terms, and stop the articles competing with it.** Not more articles.

# Task 1 — Own the "corporate" cluster on the hub

**Highest priority. Do this first.**

The hub already ranks 5.2 to 9.1 for six corporate queries and earns nothing from them. Fix the title, the meta and the on-page language so the result matches what the searcher typed.

**Current title:** `Group Health Insurance Dubai & UAE | Compare Plans from AED 2/Day*` (66 characters)
**Current meta:** `Compare & buy group health insurance in Dubai with Policybazaar UAE! Get the best group medical plans from top insurers. Starting at AED 2/day*` (143 characters)

**What to do:**

1. Work "corporate" into the title without losing "group health insurance", which the hub also ranks for. Keep it under 60 characters so it is not truncated.
2. Rewrite the meta to speak to an employer buying for staff, not to an individual. It currently reads like a consumer product page.
3. Add an H2 that uses the word **corporate** directly. Not one of the 31 existing headings contains it.
4. Add a short section answering the corporate-buyer question specifically: what a corporate plan is, how it differs from an individual policy, and who it suits.

**Target queries for this task:** corporate health insurance · corporate health plan · corporate medical insurance · corporate insurance plans · best corporate health insurance plans · affordable group health insurance for 10 employees

**How we will know it worked:** clicks from those six queries move from 0. Position is already good enough.

The hub currently loses to its own child articles on the segment's biggest queries.

| group health insurance dubai | 804 | 25.1 | **small-businesses article** | **21.3** |

The small-businesses article outranks the hub on five of these seven. It is a page about startups and small firms ranking for generic head terms, which is why nothing converts: the searcher wants the main product page and gets an SME guide.

**What to do:**

1. On the hub, make the head terms unambiguous in the H1, the opening paragraph and the H2 set. Five of the seven queries above say **medical**, not **health**. 2. On the small-businesses article, narrow the language to small business, SME and startup. Remove or rephrase generic "group medical insurance in the UAE" passages that make it compete for head terms it should not hold.
3. Add a clear link from the small-businesses article to the hub, using the head term as the anchor text.

**Do not** delete or redirect the small-businesses article. It carries 10,506 impressions and it has its own genuine cluster: `group medical insurance for small business` at 218 impressions, `small business group health insurance` at 159, `group medical insurance for smes uae` at 148.

# Task 3 — The page-1 article that earns nothing

`/group-health-insurance/articles/group-health-insurance-and-pre-existing-conditions/`
**Position 11.7 · 2,124 impressions · 2 clicks · CTR 0.09%**

This is the only article on page 1 at real volume. The content is fine; it already covers day-one cover, medical tests and exclusions, and it has FAQ schema. The snippet is the problem.

**Current title:** `Group Health Insurance and Pre-Existing Conditions: What You Need to Know` (73 characters, will truncate)

Drop "What You Need to Know" and "Discover essential information on", which use up the characters that should carry the answer.

**Target queries:** group health insurance pre existing conditions (153 impressions, position 17.3) · pre existing condition health insurance uae (87, position 21.0) · does group health insurance cover pre existing conditions (28, position 16.0)

# Task 4 — Write for the AI Overview format

AI Overviews appear on 4 of the 5 Group Health queries we track. Policybazaar is cited in 2. Every one of those overviews opens the same way, with the legal mandate:

> "legally required by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA) for all private-sector employees. Employers must provide at least the Essential Benefits Plan (EBP)"

> "legally mandatory for all private-sector employers across all seven emirates in the UAE to issue or renew employee residence visas"

> "legally mandatory for all employers across all seven emirates. Companies must provide basic medical coverage to issue or renew employee visas. Annual costs range from…"

**What to do:** add a block of 60 to 90 words near the top of the hub, `how-to-get-health-insurance-for-employees` and `business-health-insurance`, answering in this order:

1. Who is legally required to provide it
2. Under which authority
3. What the minimum plan is
4. What it costs
5. What the penalty is for not having it

Write it as a single self-contained passage that could be quoted without any surrounding context. That is the shape being cited.

The hub already states all five facts somewhere on the page. They are scattered across 31 headings rather than assembled into one quotable paragraph.

**Start with `business health insurance uae`.** That AI Overview cites **exactly one source, hayah.com**. It is the easiest citation to win. The page that should win it, `business-health-insurance`, mentions DHA but **not DOH, not MOHRE and not the Essential Benefits Plan** — and the overview is built entirely on the all-seven-emirates mandate.

# Task 5 — Rewrite the three pages that collect impressions and rank nowhere

### 5a. Abu Dhabi article — factual error

`…/group-health-insurance-in-abu-dhabi/` · **2,603 impressions · 0 clicks · position 79.9**, the worst in the segment.

**The page is about Abu Dhabi and does not mention DOH, the Abu Dhabi regulator, anywhere.** It cites DHA, which regulates Dubai. Abu Dhabi runs under the Department of Health, not the DHA Essential Benefits Plan. Rewrite around the correct authority and the correct minimum-cover rules.

It currently ranks 88.0 to 88.6 for generic UAE head terms it should not target at all, and 19.3 for `small business health insurance abu dhabi`, which is the query it should own. Narrow it to Abu Dhabi.

### 5b. Things-to-consider article

`…/things-to-consider-when-buying-group-health-insurance-uae/` · **2,489 impressions · 0 clicks · position 64.3**

Ten headings, against 21 to 31 on the pages that work. It is the **only page in the folder with no FAQ schema**. It ranks 81 to 84 for head terms and 13.2 for `uae foreign assignment group medical insurance`, 8.8 for `group protection coverage considerations for education employers in dubai` and 6.6 for `how to evaluate wellness integration in group health insurance policies`.

Those long-tail positions are the clue: the page is closer to an evaluation-criteria guide than a buying-tips list. Expand it in that direction, add an FAQ block, and stop it competing on head terms.

### 5c. Group versus individual article

`…/group-health-insurance-vs-individual-health-insurance/` · **1,655 impressions · 1 click · position 60.5**

It ranks 75.9 for `group insurance vs individual insurance`, 85.4 for `difference between group and individual insurance` and 94.3 for `is group insurance better than individual`. A comparison page ranking at 60 to 94 for its own comparison queries needs a genuine side-by-side table — cost, cover, eligibility, portability, what happens when you leave the company — not prose.

# Task 7 — Give cost a real answer

**169 queries · 1,955 impressions · 1 click · average position 49.1** — the worst-ranking theme in the segment.

The hub already has an H2, "How Much Does Group Health Insurance Cost in the UAE?", with five sub-headings on the factors that affect cost. It still ranks 49 on this cluster.

**Cost factors are not a cost answer.** Add actual AED ranges by employee band and by plan tier, each dated and attributed. Every AI Overview in this segment quotes a cost range; ours does not give one in a form anyone can quote.

The hub already ranks **7.5 for `affordable group health insurance for 10 employees`** with 205 impressions and zero clicks, so a specific, numbered cost answer has a route to page 1 immediately.

# Task 10 — Standards for everything you touch

- **Named author and reviewer.** The hub, `how-to-get-health-insurance-for-employees` and `business-health-insurance` carry Chanchal Singh and Ashu Bajaj. `group-health-insurance-and-pre-existing-conditions` has no author markup at all.
- **A visible last-updated date.**
- **The correct regulator for the emirate.** DHA for Dubai, DOH for Abu Dhabi, MOHRE for labour-law points. Getting this wrong is what is holding the Abu Dhabi page at position 79.
- **Every AED figure dated and sourced.** This is a YMYL topic and the sources being cited in AI Overviews are insurers and government.
- **Link the government source** when you state a legal requirement. `u.ae` and `iloe.ae` are both cited in the AI Overview for `employee medical insurance uae`.
- **An FAQ block with FAQ schema.** Every page in the folder has one except `things-to-consider`.
- No "not financial advice" boilerplate standing in for an actual source.
